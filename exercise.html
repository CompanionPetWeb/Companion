Life-Stage Cautions

The same activity isn't safe at every age or for every breed.

Puppies & growth plates

Avoid forced long runs, jumping, or repetitive high-impact activity until growth plates close (varies by breed size, often 12–18 months). Short, frequent play is safer than one long session.

Seniors & joints

Keep it low-impact — shorter, more frequent walks beat one long one. Watch for stiffness after activity and adjust down if it appears.

Flat-faced (brachycephalic) breeds

Bulldogs, Pugs, and similar breeds overheat fast and struggle to cool down. Keep exercise short, avoid heat and humidity entirely.

After meals

Avoid vigorous exercise right after a big meal, especially in deep-chested breeds — linked to bloat risk. Rest 1+ hour before intense activity.

General guidance only — a dog with an existing injury, condition, or breed-specific risk should have their exercise plan checked with a vet first.
